# tools/drive/lab.txt — the playable lab, plus the collision overlay.
#
#   ./dev drive petabyte-madness-debug tools/drive/lab.txt pm-lab
#
# LAB is the one section of the intro the player drives: first person, walk the
# station, read three notes, activate the MRI when you decide to. It is also
# where the collision is entirely hand-authored — dank_lab.obj carries geometry
# and no collision mesh, so pm_lab.c types six brushes plus blockers against
# the model's bounding box, and its own comment admits "every position here is
# derived from the model's bounding box, not from looking at the room… they do
# not yet put things ON the bench they are meant to be on."
#
# That is a defect you cannot see and cannot photograph. So this script's real
# payload is the last third: turn on pm_debug's `clip` layer and photograph the
# brushes against the room they are supposed to line up with.
#
# LAB is the fifth entry in the debug jump menu:
#   TITLE FILE NARRATION LAB_CINE *LAB* INTAKE CREDITS SUB BEACH PLAY
