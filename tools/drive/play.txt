# PLAY is Slice 0: one corridor from assets/pm_lab.map with three imps, a
# hellhound, two gargoyles and an overlord in it, air draining, and the veil on
# Z. It is the thinnest screen in the game and the one the whole design is
# about, so it gets a script that exercises the mechanic rather than just
# photographing the room.
#
# ── What to look for, and what is not there yet ────────────────────────
# The veil's palette-swap layer is what VEIL_DESIGN.md calls "the one idea", and
# only part of it is wired: pm_veil_update, _apply_scene, _draw_vignette and
# _demon_submit are live, so raising the veil fogs the world, rebates the far
# plane, darkens the edges, and makes the demon BODIES appear. The TLUT half —
# _bind_palette, _material_pass, _prim_alpha — has no CI4 assets to point at
# yet, so nothing changes colour. A capture here is therefore the honest
# before-picture for that work.
#
